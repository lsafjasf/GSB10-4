streamdecode 100 MiB benchmark
============================================================
streaming decode, random (incompressible) payload:
  compressed   plaintext   time(s)   MiB/s   peak RSS (MiB, absolute)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
          1 MiB         1 MiB     0.001      909             31.51
         10 MiB        10 MiB     0.009     1075             31.51
         50 MiB        50 MiB     0.043     1152             31.51
        100 MiB       100 MiB     0.087     1153             31.51

peak RSS stays flat while the response grows 1 -> 100 MiB: memory does not follow the body size.

100 MiB compressed response (decompressed = 100 MiB):
  streaming : 0.087 s, 1151 MiB/s, peak RSS 31.51 MiB
  one-shot  : 0.367 s, 272 MiB/s, peak RSS 415.28 MiB

100 MiB highly-compressible payload (compressed 12.8 MiB): 0.108 s, 923 MiB/s, peak RSS 31.51 MiB
